## Approval boundary

- Do not infer a word's correctness from an automated test passing.
- Do not mark training, redistribution or commercial use as allowed without documented permission for that exact source.
- Do not promote an entry automatically.
- Record corrections and rejected readings so they can be reproduced as stable test cases.
